# ASSIGNMENT 6

Updates:

In our design, we chose a school map as our scene layout, coinciding with the story and background of the game and the protagonist. The enemies and AI constructs consist of designs reflecting the paranormal objects that haunt the hallways and classrooms, and each enemy has its own unique AI with respect to gameplay. Furthermore, the introduction and improvement of current actions that the player can do within the school allows for more opportunities to interact with the environment, and avoid the unique paranormal objects that hunt the player, relating with the theme of the horror game.
